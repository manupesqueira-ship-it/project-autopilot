# Copywriting Standard

Product copy must be clear, specific, concise, and trustworthy.

Rules:
- CTAs must describe the action.
- Error states must tell users what happened and what to do next.
- Avoid vague hype and filler.
- Avoid exposing internal implementation details to end users.
- Keep safety, privacy, and data-use language plain.
- Match tone to product category and audience.

For MIRA, internal demo copy may mention QA mock mode. Customer-facing copy must not ask users to understand infrastructure.
