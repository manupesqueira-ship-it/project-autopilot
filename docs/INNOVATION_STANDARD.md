# Innovation Standard

Project Autopilot should encourage originality without confusing users.

Reward:
- Interaction ideas that make the product easier to understand.
- Visual identity that feels specific to the product.
- Strong moments of delight that do not slow the workflow.
- Clear editorial or product storytelling where appropriate.

Penalize:
- Gimmicks that reduce usability.
- Trend copying without product relevance.
- Decorative complexity that hides the main action.
- Generic templates that could belong to any SaaS landing page.

Innovation is not novelty alone. It is a useful, memorable product experience.
