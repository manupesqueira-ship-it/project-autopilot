# Design Rubric

Score every UI/design task across these categories:

1. Visual hierarchy
2. Spacing rhythm
3. Typography consistency
4. Color discipline
5. Contrast/accessibility
6. CTA clarity
7. Mobile responsiveness
8. Desktop polish
9. Interaction feedback
10. Loading/error states
11. Originality/innovation
12. Premium feel
13. Brand coherence
14. Copywriting clarity
15. Flow friction
16. Emotional pull
17. Novelty without gimmicks
18. Information density
19. Trust and credibility
20. Product-market tone fit

Passing design means the experience is not merely functional. It must feel intentional, legible, polished, and appropriate to the product category.
